Blue+ Face Serum Creative Content Guide

Discover how to make the most of Qure's Blue+ Face Serum with our creative content guide.

This brief will help you craft engaging content that shows how Blue+ works with the Q-Rejuvalight Pro LED Mask to amplify results from every LED session.

Contents

Product Information

Blue+ is a lightweight, LED‑enhancing serum designed to be applied before an LED session, working with your Q‑Rejuvalight Pro to help skin respond more effectively to light — without increasing intensity, heat, or irritation.

Hero active:

  • USP Methylene Blue (0.1%) - a cosmetic photosensitizer that helps skin absorb and convert LED light into stronger cellular signaling, amplifying results from both Skin-Clearing (blue light) and Skin-Rejuvenating (red light) modes.

Supporting actives:

  • Calendula iActive soothes and comforts skin, with added antioxidant support for skin exposed to daily screen/blue light
  • Niacinamide supports a healthy-looking barrier and more even-looking tone
  • Beta-Glucan helps soothe the appearance of skin
  • Sodium Hyaluronate delivers lightweight hydration
  • Bisabolol + Allantoin support skin comfort
  • Vitamin E (Tocopheryl Acetate) provides antioxidant support
Learn more about Qure Blue+ Face Serum

Usage Instructions

How To Use (with Q-Rejuvalight Pro LED Mask):

  1. Cleanse skin thoroughly.
  2. Apply a pea-sized amount evenly to face and neck.
  3. Allow a few minutes for absorption.
  4. Apply the Q-Rejuvalight Pro LED Mask and run your selected treatment mode.
  5. Do not rinse off — continue with your regular skincare routine after thse LED session.

Note: excess product may cause temporary staining — a pea-sized amount is all you need.

How To Use (on its own):

  1. Cleanse skin thoroughly.
  2. Apply a pea-sized amount evenly to face and neck.
  3. Massage in until fully absorbed.
  4. Follow with your regular moisturizer or next skincare step.

Can be used daily, with or without your LED session

Creative Direction

Recording Guidelines

  • Use bright, natural lighting. No filters.
  • Please do not wear makeup (unless to show makeup removal).
  • Show the full routine: applying Blue+, then putting on the Q-Rejuvalight Pro LED Mask.
  • Keep backgrounds clean and uncluttered.
  • In cover photos, always show the serum in use (unless agreed otherwise).
  • Because the serum has a blue tint, a close-up of it on skin or on the applicator makes for a strong visual — feel free to highlight this.
