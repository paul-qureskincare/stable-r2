Our Skin Clearing Mode combines blue and red light wavelengths. Blue (415 nm) helps with mild-to-moderate acne, while red (630 nm) helps calm and soothe the appearance of irritated skin.

Together, these wavelengths help improve the appearance of breakouts, support clearer-looking skin, and promote a balanced, healthier-looking complexion.

  • Blue (415 nm)
  • Red (630 nm)
