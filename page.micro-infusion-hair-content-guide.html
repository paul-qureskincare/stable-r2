Usage Instructions

Weekly Treatment (device):

  1. Perform your Micro-Infusion Pro Hair treatment once a week, using a fresh, sterile, single-use device each session.
  2. On the nights between treatments, apply a few drops of Hair Renewal Serum, massaged into and around the scalp.

Each device is sterile and single-use only. Do not reuse a device across sessions.

Content Requirements

Hair is a more heavily regulated category than skin. Hair regrowth is treated as a drug claim in the US, and this space has been actively pursued by the FTC for unsubstantiated regrowth claims. Read the do-not-say list below carefully, it applies to you as the creator, not just to Qure's own marketing.

Messaging must include:

  • This is a weekly at-home treatment, performed with a fresh, sterile, single-use device each session.
  • Results build with consistent use over several weeks, never framed as fast or immediate.
  • The Hair Renewal Serum is used nightly on non-infusion nights, separate from the weekly device treatment.
  • Only ever describe visible appearance, such as "visibly fuller, denser-looking hair," never hair growth, hair count, or follicle activity.

Never say:

  • Any medical condition name: alopecia, pattern baldness, androgenetic alopecia, or "hair loss" as something being treated.
  • Anything implying a biological or structural change: "regrows hair," "stimulates follicles," "reactivates dormant follicles," "increases hair density," "boosts circulation," "restores hair growth cycle," "my hair grew back," "my bald spot filled in."
  • Any speed or immediacy claim: "instant results," "overnight thickness," "see results after one use," "immediate improvement."
  • Any in-clinic or professional-treatment comparison, for this product this comparison is off-limits under any framing.
  • Any unhedged comparison to derma rollers or other at-home devices, such as "safer than" or "better than" without "typically."
  • The overpromise: "full head of hair," "cure baldness," "reverse hair loss," "regrow lost hair," "get your hair back."

If you want to talk about the device sanitation or hygiene versus reusable tools, the only approved phrasing is: “Typically more hygienic than reusable derma rollers, since each tip is sterile and single-use.”

All hair-related content is reviewed before it goes live, this is treated as Qure's own advertising once it's paid or gifted, so accuracy here matters as much as it would coming from us directly.

Remember to add a personal touch to the messaging, focused on how the ritual feels and looks, not on any biological claim you can't back up.

Examples:

  • “This is my Tuesday night ritual now.”
  • “My hair looks visibly fuller after a few weeks of doing this every week.”

Recording Guidelines

  • Use bright, natural lighting. No filters.
  • Show the full routine: prepping a fresh device, performing the weekly treatment, and applying the Hair Renewal Serum on an off night.
  • Keep backgrounds clean and uncluttered.
  • In cover photos, always show the device or serum in use (unless agreed otherwise).
  • Close-ups of the device's sterile packaging or the gold-plated needle head make for a strong, credible visual.
