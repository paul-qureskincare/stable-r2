Have questions?

What is the Hair Renewal Serum?

A daily scalp serum with pea sprout extract, peptides and niacinamide, designed to support the appearance of fuller, denser-looking hair over time. The 60 mL dropper bottle makes it easy to apply directly to targeted areas of the scalp.

What are the benefits?

With consistent use, the Hair Renewal Serum may help with:

  • The look of thinning areas
  • Visibly fuller, denser-looking hair
  • A hydrated, conditioned scalp
  • A refreshing, invigorating scalp feel
Who is it for?

Anyone who wants fuller, denser-looking hair, especially if you’ve noticed the appearance of thinning around the part, crown or hairline.

How do I use it?

Apply a few drops directly onto a clean, dry scalp and gently massage into and around the area.

Where on the scalp should I apply it?

Focus on areas where you’ve noticed the appearance of thinning, such as the part line, crown or hairline, then gently massage into and around the scalp.

How often should I use it?

Apply nightly. If you’re also using the Micro-Infusion Pro Hair System, skip the serum on your weekly treatment night.

Do I need to use it every day?

For best visible results, yes — apart from your weekly Micro-Infusion treatment night, if you’re using both products together. The appearance of fuller, denser-looking hair takes time, so consistent use is important.

What if I miss a day?

No problem. Continue with your next application as usual. There’s no need to double up.

How quickly will I see results?

Visible changes take time. The Hair Renewal Serum is designed to support the appearance of fuller, denser-looking hair over several weeks of consistent daily use.

Can I use it with the Q-Renew Helmet?

Yes. They’re designed to work together. Use the Q-Renew Helmet first on a clean, dry scalp, then apply the Hair Renewal Serum.

Can I use it with the Micro-Infusion Pro Hair System?

Yes. Apply the Hair Renewal Serum nightly on non-infusion nights. Skip it on your weekly Micro-Infusion treatment day to keep the scalp conditioned and cared for between treatments.

What’s the difference between the Hair Renewal Serum and the Micro-Infusion Hair Serum?

Both formulas share the same core actives. The Micro-Infusion Hair Serum is formulated for your weekly Micro-Infusion treatment, while the Hair Renewal Serum is your daily scalp step and also contains peppermint oil for a cooling, invigorating feel.

I’ve tried other hair products before. What makes this different?

It combines several key actives in one lightweight daily step: pea sprout extract to target the look of thinning, peptides and clover flower extract to support the appearance of scalp vitality and hair fullness, and niacinamide for daily scalp conditioning and hydration.

What are the key ingredients?

Pea Sprout Extract: Helps target the look of thinning for denser-looking hair
Acetyl Tetrapeptide-3 & Clover Flower Extract: Help support the appearance of scalp vitality and hair fullness
Niacinamide: Helps condition and hydrate the scalp
Caffeine & Peppermint Oil: Provide an invigorating, cooling scalp sensation

Why does the serum contain peppermint oil?

Peppermint oil provides a refreshing, cooling sensation that makes the daily scalp ritual feel invigorating. It is included only in the Hair Renewal Serum, not in the Micro-Infusion Hair Serum.

What does it feel like on the scalp?

Peppermint oil and caffeine provide a cooling, invigorating sensation. A light tingling sensation may occur on application.

Will it make my hair look greasy?

The lightweight formula is designed for easy daily scalp application and should not weigh hair down.

Can I use it on color-treated hair?

Yes. The serum can be used on color-treated hair and does not interfere with hair color.

How long does one bottle last?

One 60 mL bottle should last approximately 3 months with regular daily use.

Is it safe to use?

The formula has been dermatologically tested for skin and scalp tolerability.* As with any new product, patch test first and stop use if irritation occurs.
