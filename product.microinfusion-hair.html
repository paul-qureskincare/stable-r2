Have questions?

What is the Micro-Infusion Pro Hair System?

An at-home scalp treatment that pairs virtually painless micro-stamping technology with a targeted hair serum. It creates precise micro-channels at the scalp surface for targeted serum delivery, supporting the appearance of fuller, denser-looking hair over time.

What are the benefits?

With consistent use, the Micro-Infusion Pro Hair System may help support:

  • The appearance of thinning areas
  • Visibly fuller, denser-looking hair
  • The appearance of scalp vitality and hair fullness
  • A conditioned, hydrated scalp
Who is it for?

Anyone looking to support the appearance of fuller, denser-looking hair, especially if you’ve noticed the look of thinning around the part, crown or hairline.

How do I use it?

Perform the treatment once a week on a clean scalp. Focus on areas where hair appears thinner and use the preassembled device as directed.

How often should I use it?

Once weekly. On non-infusion nights, you can use the Hair Renewal Serum as part of your regular scalp-care routine.

Does it hurt?

The micro-stamping treatment is virtually painless. If your scalp is particularly sensitive or you experience discomfort, use lighter pressure and stamp more gently. Stop the treatment if discomfort persists.

What’s included in the kit?

Each preassembled, single-use device contains 4 mL of Micro-Infusion Hair Serum and an EO-sterilized needle head with 20 × 0.5 mm microneedles.

Do I need the Hair Renewal Serum?

The Hair Renewal Serum is sold separately and is designed to complement your weekly Micro-Infusion treatment. Use it nightly on non-infusion nights to keep the scalp conditioned and hydrated while supporting the appearance of fuller, denser-looking hair between treatments.

Can I use it with the Q-Renew Helmet?

Yes. The Q-Renew Helmet and Micro-Infusion Pro Hair System can both be incorporated into your hair-care routine. Follow the individual directions for each product.

What’s the difference between the Micro-Infusion Hair Serum and the Hair Renewal Serum?

Both formulas share the same core actives. The Micro-Infusion Hair Serum is formulated specifically for your weekly Micro-Infusion treatment.

The Hair Renewal Serum is your nightly scalp serum for non-infusion nights and also contains peppermint oil for a cooling, invigorating sensation.

What are the key ingredients?
  • Pea Sprout Extract: Supports the appearance of denser, fuller-looking hair over time
  • Acetyl Tetrapeptide-3 & Clover Flower Extract: Help support the appearance of scalp vitality and hair fullness
  • Niacinamide: Helps condition and hydrate the scalp
  • Caffeine: Known for its invigorating feel on the scalp
  • Copper Tripeptide-1: A conditioning, antioxidant-rich ingredient used in scalp and hair care
How quickly will I see results?

Visible changes take time. The system is designed to support the appearance of fuller, denser-looking hair with consistent use over time.

Is the Micro-Infusion Pro Hair System reusable?

No. Each device is designed for a single treatment and should be disposed of after use, giving you a fresh needle head for every weekly treatment.

Is it safe to use?

Each treatment uses an EO-sterilized, single-use needle head. The serum formula has also been dermatologically tested for skin and scalp tolerability. Follow the directions and warnings provided, patch test before first use and discontinue use if irritation occurs.
