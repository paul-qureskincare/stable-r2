How It Works

Apply immediately after Micro-Infusion treatment.

  1. Remove the mask from its pouch and carefully unfold.
  2. Apply directly to the face after Micro-Infusion, without cleansing or drying the skin first.
  3. Leave on for 15-20 minutes.
  4. Remove the mask and gently massage any remaining serum into the skin.
  5. Do not rinse. Leave the remaining serum on overnight.
  6. Follow with your regular cleanser the next morning.

Pro tip: Store the sealed pouch in the refrigerator before use for an enhanced cooling effect.
