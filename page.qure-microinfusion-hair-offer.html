Have questions?

What's included in the Two-Step Hair System?

The 3-Month Kit includes 6 Micro-Infusion Pro Hair System devices, each with a sterile, single-use tip and 4 mL of infusion serum, plus a 60 mL Hair Renewal Serum, free. Use the device for your treatment and the serum on the nights in between.

How does subscription billing work?

Your subscription renews every 1 or 3 months, depending on the plan you choose, and ships automatically. You can pause, skip or cancel anytime in your account.

Can I use the Micro-Infusion Pro Hair System without the Hair Renewal Serum?

Yes. Each treatment comes with its own infusion serum, so the device works on its own. For the complete routine, we recommend adding the Hair Renewal Serum on the nights in between.

Is the Micro-Infusion Pro Hair System compatible with other hair treatments?

We recommend starting with the system on its own. If you use other scalp or hair treatments, avoid applying them on your Micro-Infusion treatment day. If you use prescription treatments, check with your dermatologist first.

How soon will I see results?

Visible changes take time. The system is designed to support the appearance of fuller, denser-looking hair over several weeks of consistent use.

Is this suitable for sensitive scalps?

Patch test first. Don't use on irritated, broken or sunburned skin, and stop use if irritation occurs. If you have a scalp condition or are unsure, check with your dermatologist.

Will I feel anything during the Micro-Infusion treatment?

The micro-stamping is designed to be virtually painless. You may feel light pressure on the scalp, and a mild tingling is possible

Peppermint Oil Notice

The Hair Renewal Serum contains peppermint oil for a cooling, invigorating feel, and a light tingling sensation may occur on application. The infusion serum used with the device does not contain peppermint oil. Avoid contact with eyes, and stop use if irritation occurs.

How often should I use the Hair Renewal Serum?

Nightly on non-infusion nights. On your weekly Micro-Infusion treatment day, skip the serum, since the infusion serum covers that day.

Do I need to wait before applying other products after using the Hair Renewal Serum?

Apply the serum to a clean, dry scalp and let it absorb before applying other products.

How is this different from Minoxidil or hair supplements?

The Two-Step Hair System is a cosmetic scalp routine, not a drug or a supplement. It's designed to support the appearance of fuller, denser-looking hair with consistent use, and it is not intended to treat hair loss.
