Have questions?

Is this serum suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes. Blue+ Face Serum is dermatologically tested, non-irritating, hypoallergenic, and suitable for sensitive skin. The formula includes soothing and barrier-supporting ingredients such as beta-glucan, bisabolol, allantoin, and aloe to help keep skin comfortable.

Does Blue+ work without LED?

Yes. Blue+ can be used on its own as a lightweight antioxidant serum that helps support skin exposed to everyday environmental stressors, including screen-based blue light. When used with LED, it is designed to enhance how the skin responds to red and blue light.

Will this replace my other serums?

Not necessarily. Blue+ is designed to work as a targeted treatment step. It can be used on its own as a simple, all-in-one antioxidant serum, or layered with other products depending on your routine and skin needs.

How often should I use it?

Blue+ can be used daily. When using LED, apply it before each LED session. When not using LED, apply in the morning as part of your skincare routine to help support skin exposed to daily environmental stressors.

How much should I use?

It is important to use only a pea-sized amount for the face and neck. Applying more than this is not necessary and may lead to temporary blue surface staining.

Always allow the serum to fully absorb for a few minutes before applying your LED mask or other products. Blue+ has been tested with the Q-Rejuvalight Pro LED Mask materials and is designed to absorb without interfering with light coverage. Any temporary tinting will fade on its own.

Does it work with both red and blue LED modes?

Yes. Blue+ was formulated to work with both red and blue LED wavelengths. The formula uses methylene blue to absorb light and support the skin’s biological response, helping blue light target acne-causing bacteria and red light support skin renewal and overall skin quality.

How does the refill system work?

The refill system is designed so you keep the outer bottle and pump, and only replace the inner serum refill. To refill, detach the outer case from the base, remove the empty refill, slide in the new refill, then place the case back on and twist the base to open and unlock the nozzle. This reduces waste while keeping the same packaging experience.
