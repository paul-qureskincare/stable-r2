This brief will help you elevate your skincare routine with the Qure Dermal Mist. Powered by hypochlorous acid spray and designed to prevent breakouts, this mist can be used to reduce impurities during the day or cleanse before your favorite Micro-Infusion treatment.

Usage Instructions

How To Use:

  1. Post-workout essential: Spray right after a gym session to help keep breakouts at bay before you can get to a full cleanse.
  2. Post-workout essential: In between washes, a spray on your pillowcase can help keep breakout-causing germs under control.
  3. Pre-treatments purifier: Before Micro-Infusion, give your skin a spritz to cleanse and prep for better results.
  4. Device defender: After using your Q-Rejuvalight Pro, a light spray and wipe inside your device keeps it clean.

Product Claims

Key claims:

  • Reduces impurities and prevents breakouts without stripping or drying out the skin.
  • Accelerates skin healing post-treatment or during flare-ups.
  • Use on-the-go to keep skin calm and clear - no rising needed.
  • Dermatologically tested, versatile and safe for use on your face, body, and devices.
  • Safe for sensitive skin and gentle enough for daily use.
  • Travel-friendly breakout control, available in two sizes.

Content Requirements

Messaging must include:

  • Light and calming spray, and doesn't leave skin sticky or tight.
  • Soothes redness and irritation without stinging or dryness.
  • Just mist and go — no rinsing, no mess.
  • Use after cleansing, post-workout, during travel, or anytime skin feels off.
  • 100ml for everyday use, 15ml for on-the-go breakout control.
  • Gentle enough for sensitive or reactive skin.

Remember to add a personal touch to the messaging, talk about how it makes your skin feel and when you use it.

Examples:

  • “My skin flares up after the gym, and this calms it down instantly.”
  • “I always keep this in my bag to use when I'm traveling.”
  • “It feels so light and refreshing, but makes my skin feel clean and refreshed.”
